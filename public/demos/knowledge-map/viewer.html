Knowledge Map

Important — please read before you start

The Knowledge Map is experimental, unfinished test software, shared for evaluation only. It looks up publicly available information about a place — right now, from Wikipedia — and reads it back to you.

  • It can be wrong or out of date. The information comes from Wikipedia and may be inaccurate, incomplete, or stale. Everything is labelled with its source and how recently it was fetched — weigh it accordingly.
  • It is not a navigation or mobility aid. It describes what is known about an area, not how to get anywhere. Keep using your usual ways of getting around.
  • Your location and searches are sent to the server to look up the area, and the server fetches matching public articles from Wikipedia.
